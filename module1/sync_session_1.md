# Sync Session 1: Orientation and Environment Check

**60 minutes · live on Zoom · recorded**
**When:** [TO CONFIRM — date, time, and Zoom link on Canvas]

## Purpose

Get every student to a working environment, and set expectations for how AI is
used in this course.

If you cannot attend live, **you must watch the recording**. Environment problems
must be resolved before Module 2 begins.

## Agenda

| # | Item | Time |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Welcome and introductions | 10 min |
| 2 | How this course is sequenced, and why the first two modules are AI-free | 10 min |
| 3 | Walkthrough of the AI-Use Policy and the four permission levels, with questions | 15 min |
| 4 | Live environment troubleshooting — students share screens as needed | 20 min |
| 5 | What to expect in Module 2 | 5 min |

## How to prepare

- **Attempt the environment setup beforehand** using
  [`setup/SETUP_GUIDE.md`](../setup/SETUP_GUIDE.md) or the setup screencast.
- **Bring any error messages.** Run `python setup/verify_environment.py` and have
  its output ready to share. Copy the text rather than taking a photo.
- **Read the [AI-Use Policy](../welcome/06_ai_use_policy.md)** so you can bring
  questions to item 3.
