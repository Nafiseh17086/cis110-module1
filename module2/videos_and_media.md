## Tracing a Loop by Hand
**Module 2 · 5–6 minutes · [Watch on YouTube](https://youtu.be/BqiXFT9rDJI)**

Filmed on paper, not in a code editor — because tracing is something you do
away from the computer. The video works through a nested loop one row at a
time, filling in a full trace table.

Watch for the moment where your instructor pauses and corrects a mistake. That
is deliberate: noticing your own error mid-trace is the skill.

**Follow along:** the program and a blank trace table are in
`trace_table_template.md`. Print it, or fill it in as you watch.

**Takeaway:** you can predict what a program will do before you run it.
